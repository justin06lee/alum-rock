Your first visit · Su primera visita

About an hour, and no surprises

  1. Call or book online. Tell us what's going on. If it hurts, you come in today.
  2. Paperwork in your language. Forms in English, Spanish and Vietnamese, on a tablet at the front desk or on your phone before you arrive.
  3. Exam, X-rays and cleaning. Digital X-rays use a fraction of the radiation of film. You'll see every photo on the screen next to the chair.
  4. A plan with prices. What needs doing now, what can wait, and what it costs with your insurance. Then you decide.

What to bring

  • A list of your medicines
  • Old X-rays, if you have them

Emergencies · Emergencias dentales

Toothache or broken tooth? Call now.

We keep time open every day for emergencies, Saturdays included. Call when we open and you'll usually be seen that morning.

Go to the emergency room or call 911 if your face or jaw is swelling fast, you have a fever with the swelling, or you're having trouble breathing or swallowing.

What to do on the way

A knocked-out adult tooth

Pick it up by the crown (the part you chew with), not the root. If it's dirty, rinse it in milk or water for a few seconds without scrubbing. Push it gently back into the socket and bite on a clean cloth. If you can't, keep it in a cup of milk. Get here within 30 minutes if you can. That gives the tooth its best chance.

Baby teeth shouldn't be put back. Bring your child in instead.

A bad toothache

Rinse with warm salt water and floss gently around the tooth in case something is stuck. Hold a cold pack on your cheek. Take a pain reliever as the label says, but don't put aspirin on your gum: it can burn it.

A chipped or broken tooth

Save any pieces, rinse your mouth with warm water, and use a cold pack for swelling. Avoid chewing on that side until we've seen it.

A crown or filling came out

Keep the crown and bring it with you. Don't glue it back with household glue. A little sugar-free gum or dental wax over the spot can help until your visit.
