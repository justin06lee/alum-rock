The coffee side of Soulsip

Smooth, creamy, bold, with a little Soulsip twist.

From rich coffee flavors to creamy specialty creations, there's a cup waiting for every coffee lover. Start with a Vietnamese coffee dripped through a phin, or pick one of nine foam coffees: coconut, pandan, brown sugar, egg, tiramisu, sesame, ube, sea salt or cheese creama.

Bring your coffee besties and try something new together.
