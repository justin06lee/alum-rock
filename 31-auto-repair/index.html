How a visit works

  1. Drop off or wait. Coffee, Wi-Fi and a clean waiting room, or a free shuttle home or to work.
  2. Digital inspection. Your tech photographs anything worn, leaking or broken and texts you a link: green, yellow, red.
  3. You approve the work. Line-item prices for each repair. Say yes to some, all, or none — no pressure either way.
  4. We show you the old parts. In a bag on the passenger seat, unless you tell us to toss them.
  5. Pick up and pay. Keys, invoice and warranty in hand. We'll text a reminder when the next service is due.
