Before you call

How to read your tire size

It's molded into the sidewall of your tire, and printed on the sticker inside the driver's door jamb. Here's what every part of 225/65R17 102H means.

Truck tire written like 33x12.50R20? That's 33 inches tall, 12.5 inches wide, for a 20-inch wheel.

P or LT in front? P is a passenger tire. LT is a light-truck tire with a stiffer sidewall. Don't mix the two on the same axle.
