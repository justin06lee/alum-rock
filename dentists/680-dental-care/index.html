Your dentist

Meet Dr. V

680 Dental Care is a dental office in San Jose, the heart of Silicon Valley, here to serve the San Jose community and beyond. The office is mostly paperless and uses digital x-rays.

Dr. Edegar Vergara, DMD

General dentist · “Dr. V”

Dr. Vergara completed both his undergraduate and dental training at Temple University in Philadelphia, PA, and his residency at St. Vincent’s Medical Charity Hospital (a Case Western University teaching hospital) in Cleveland, OH. For your convenience, he provides most procedures in-house. Outside the office, Dr. V enjoys discovering hiking trails around the Bay Area, traveling, and spending time with his family.
