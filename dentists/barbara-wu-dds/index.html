Services

Beauty, health, function

The practice provides implants, crowns, bridges, mouthguards, Invisalign, veneers and teeth whitening, as well as other aspects of cosmetic and general dentistry.

Checkups & cleanings

Prevention comes first: routine checkups and cleanings keep teeth healthy and lasting.

Dental implants

A permanent base for a replacement tooth, bridge or denture. Dr. Wu is trained in implant dentistry by Nobel Biocare.

Crowns & bridges

To protect a weak tooth, or replace a missing one by anchoring to the teeth beside it.

Dentures

Full and partial dentures.

Full-mouth reconstruction

Bridges, dentures and implants to restore form and function to the bite.

Invisalign

Clear aligners for mild and moderate crowding, spacing and crossbite.

Porcelain veneers

For stained, chipped, spaced or crooked teeth. Come in for a consultation.

Teeth whitening

In the office in one sitting, or at home with custom bleaching trays and gel.

Sports mouth guards

Custom-made to protect teeth during contact sports.

Your dentist

Dr. Barbara Wu

Dr. Barbara Wu, DDS

General, family and cosmetic dentist

Dr. Wu is a graduate of the University of California San Francisco School of Dentistry and completed Advanced Education in General Dentistry at the University of the Pacific Arthur A. Dugoni School of Dentistry. She practiced in the San Francisco Bay Area before starting her own practice in north San Jose.

“I am committed to listening to the concerns of my patients and providing the best care for my patients. … As a fitness enthusiast and dentist, I understand how oral health affects systemic health and how preventative health care is the key to long lasting health. … I started this practice with the belief that I can restore health, function, and beauty to your teeth, and therefore provide you better confidence and better overall health. Let me be your advocate and partner for health.”

Barbara Wu, DDS
