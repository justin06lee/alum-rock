About us

A cosmetic and general dental office

Genie Dental Group is here to make all your wishes for a beautiful and healthy smile come true. We are a cosmetic and general dental office. We do all phases of preventive dental treatment, such as exams, digital x-ray and cleanings. We do extractions, both simple and surgical, wisdom teeth extractions, and root canals.

Our office is dedicated to help you create a healthy and amazing smile. … So come in and get that smile that you've always deserved!
