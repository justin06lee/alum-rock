About the practice

In the office's own words

“Here at NewGen Dental, our mission is to provide a uniquely, extraordinary dental experience, or what we like to call ‘dentistry with a personal touch’. Our vision is to create a practice that delivers exceptional state-of-the-art dental care and beautiful smiles while making a positive impact on our community. We want to build lifelong patient relationships, so that you and your family feel comfortable and enjoy every visit at our office.”

  • Bilingual staff. Helpful and knowledgeable, fluent in Spanish or Vietnamese.
  • A comfortable wait. A large waiting room with a TV and a tropical fish tank.
  • Here since 2014. Construction started in November 2013 and the office opened in June 2014.
