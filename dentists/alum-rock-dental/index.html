Services

What we offer

General dentistry

Dental hygiene visits, root canal therapy, emergency dentistry, and mouth guards and bite splints.

Restorative dentistry

Inlays and onlays, crowns, tooth-colored fillings, bridges, and dentures and partials.

Implant dentistry

Standard implants, mini implants, and mini implants that hold dentures in place.

Surgical dentistry

Implant placement, wisdom teeth removal, tooth extractions, and bone and gum grafting.

Cosmetic dentistry

Porcelain veneers, teeth whitening, dental bonding and tooth-colored fillings.

Six Month Smiles

“A modified orthodontic treatment that uses nearly invisible clear braces to gently straighten teeth in an average time of six months.”

Your dentist

Meet Dr. Villano

Dr. Sheila Villano has been a licensed general dentist in California since 1994. She graduated from the University of the East in Manila, Philippines, in 1990, then came to the United States to pursue her career in dentistry.

She loves doing surgery in her practice, like wisdom teeth removal. Besides that, she does root canals, cosmetic work, dentures, crowns, restorative work and short-term braces, and she regularly goes on dental missions in the Philippines.
