About the practice

Family-owned, for over two decades

For over two decades, Vivian Dental Care, a family-owned dental practice located in the heart of San Jose, California, has welcomed patients of all ages into its warm and inviting environment.

Our compassionate team of professionals provides comprehensive dental care tailored to meet the unique needs of each individual, ensuring a comfortable and stress-free experience from start to finish.
