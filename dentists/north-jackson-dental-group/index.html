Services

What we do

Should a dental emergency occur, we make every effort to see and care for you as soon as possible.

New patient exams

A thorough exam of your teeth and gums, with digital X-rays when they're needed.

Brushing & flossing

Home-care tips to keep your teeth and gums healthy between visits.

Composite fillings

Tooth-colored fillings for cavities and small chips.

Crowns & bridges

To protect a cracked or weak tooth, or fill a gap.

Root canal therapy

To save a tooth when the nerve inside is infected.

Wisdom teeth

Checking wisdom teeth and taking them out when they need to go.

Full & partial dentures

To replace missing teeth, a few or a full arch.

Veneers

Thin porcelain shells that reshape or brighten front teeth.

Teeth whitening

To lift stains and brighten your smile.
