How a boot should fit ↓

Choosing a pair

Toe, heel & width

Three separate choices. Here's what each one means, so you know what to ask for.

La horma Toe shape

El tacón Heel

El ancho Width

  • Men's: D is the standard width, EE is wide, and many work boots come in EEE.
  • Women's: B is the standard width, C is wide.
  • If the ball of your foot rolls over the edge of the sole, try a wider width, not a longer size.

Cómo se siente How it should feel

  1. Snug over the instep. That's what holds a boot on. Expect a firm pull and a little pop as your heel drops in.
  2. A little heel slip. Some lift at the heel is normal in a new boot and eases as the sole breaks in.
  3. Ball of the foot at the widest point, where the boot is meant to bend.
  4. Toes free. Room to wiggle, never pressed against the end.
  5. Try them on later in the day, with the socks you'll actually wear.
